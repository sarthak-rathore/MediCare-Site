About the Doctor

Dr. Arjun Sharma MD, FACP

Internal Medicine & Chronic Disease Specialist

With over 15 years of clinical excellence, Dr. Arjun Sharma combines the precision of modern medicine with genuine patient-centric care. A graduate of AIIMS Delhi and KEM Mumbai, he has treated over 15,000 patients and published research in leading medical journals.

Specialising in complex internal medicine cases, diabetes management, hypertension, and preventive healthcare, Dr. Sharma is committed to making world-class care accessible to every patient who walks through the doors of MediCare Clinic.
